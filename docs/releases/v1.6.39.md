## English

- Russian is now available in Settings, joining English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Korean and French. Menus, toolbars, dialogs and the tray follow the selected language, and the choice is remembered across windows.

## 中文

- 设置中新增俄语，与英语、简体中文、繁体中文、日语、德语、韩语和法语并列。菜单、工具栏、对话框和托盘会跟随所选语言，并在所有窗口间保留该选择。
